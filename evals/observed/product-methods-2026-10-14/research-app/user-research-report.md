The apparent tension between an external-copy need and objection to automatic cloud sharing is decision-relevant: export and sharing are distinct actions. A controlled, user-initiated handoff might satisfy both needs, but that is an unvalidated solution hypothesis. Existing export may be sufficient once found; a moderator/task effect or prototype limitation may explain P03's difficulty. There is no direct evidence about content format, default settings, security implementation, actual sharing behavior, feasibility, commercial desirability or price acceptance.
